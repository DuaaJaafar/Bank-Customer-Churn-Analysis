## 🎯 Business Problem & Objective
Customer retention is vital for sustainable banking operations. This project aims to identify the underlying patterns causing customers to leave the bank (churn), evaluate risk factors across demographics and account activity, and provide strategic recommendations to minimize churn rates.

---

## 🔑 Key Insights Uncovered
* *Customer Complaints:* A massive correlation exists between churn and customer complaints—almost all customers who filed a complaint ended up churning.
* *Age Distribution:* Customers in middle-to-higher age groups show significantly higher churn rates compared to younger cohorts.
* *Product Engagement:* Customers holding single products or more than 2 products exhibit higher churn vulnerability, while holding 2 products represents the most stable retention group.
* *Activity Status:* Inactive customers have a much higher likelihood of leaving the bank compared to active account holders.
* *Geographical Trends:* Germany shows a comparatively higher churn percentage compared to France and Spain.

---

## 🛠️ Excel Skills & Features Applied
* *Data Cleaning & Modeling:* Handled missing values, formatted data types, and created logical age grouping brackets.
* *Pivot Tables & Calculations:* Built dynamic aggregated summaries for multi-dimensional analysis.
* *Interactive Slicers:* Connected synchronized slicers for seamless dynamic filtering across countries, gender, and activity status.
* *UI/UX & Visual Design:* Implemented a modern dark-green theme with balanced visual hierarchy, standardized KPI alignments, and clear custom legends.
